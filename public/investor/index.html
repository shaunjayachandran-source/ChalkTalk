Investor Overview
ChalkTalk

The intelligence layer between how a team plays and how its athletes learn.

ChalkTalk starts with the interactive playbook and expands into a structured system for capturing, teaching, reinforcing and ultimately measuring team knowledge.

"Where other platforms teach you a new way to draw your plays, ChalkTalk already knows what you mean and gives you the finished playbook — in the time it takes to describe it."

Wedge: basketball programs ▲ Core product: interactive teaching system ▲ Expansion: AI, wearables, multi-sport

The Problem

Team Knowledge Is Fragmented.

Coaches know the system. Players need to learn it. Film shows what happened. But the tools used to teach, review and execute that system remain disconnected.

Static playbooks

PDFs and screenshots show the picture, but rarely teach the sequence, reads or counters.

Film lives somewhere else

Video captures execution but is not structurally connected to the team's playbook.

Teaching is hard to scale

The same coaching details are repeated in film room, practice and individual conversations.

Players learn differently

The system needs to travel beyond the whiteboard and into the athlete's daily workflow.

The Product Today

Teach The Play. Not Just The Diagram.

Entry Pass — 1 to 4, Cut and Clear

1 enters to 4 at the elbow, then 2 clears to the corner on a timing rule, not a read. Every role on the floor is coded to the player who has to execute it — not just drawn as an arrow.

1
Player 1 — Point Guard

Enters to 4 at the elbow, then cuts through immediately to fill the wing 2 is vacating. Pass and fill — the passer never stands.

Coach's Eye

Against a defense that jumps to deny 1's cut, the counter is a back-cut instead of a fill — the defender has to open up to see the ball, and 1 goes behind them to the rim.

Why Basketball First

A Focused Wedge With Platform Potential.

Basketball combines small rosters, repeatable sets, strong video availability, complex spatial relationships and a deeply ingrained coaching culture built around diagrams and terminology.

The goal is not to become a generic multi-sport app too early. The goal is to prove the teaching model in basketball, then extend the underlying system into adjacent sports where the same structure exists.

Why ChalkTalk

The Bench Is The Audience

Your offense doesn't live in a binder. It lives in the heads of fifteen people who all need to see the same thing the same way. ChalkTalk gives a coach one place to put the system, and gives everyone else a way to actually find it.
