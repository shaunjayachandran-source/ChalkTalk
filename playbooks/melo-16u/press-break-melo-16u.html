Melo 16U · Zone Press Break

Full-Court Press Break vs Zone Press

6 Phases

Phase 1 — Initial Alignment

1
3 takes the ball out of bounds at the top of the court, RIGHT side of the basket. You are the trigger — nothing moves until you signal.
2
4 sets at the top of the free throw line, center of the lane. Primary outlet — face the inbounder and be a large, stationary target.
3
1 and 2 align at the 3-point line, one on each side of 4. Stay wide — your width forces the press to cover too much ground.
4
5 sprints to the opponent's free throw line, elbow opposite of the ball. Safety valve ahead of the press. If the ball moves, you move.
Structural Bridge → Phase 2Once 3 signals ready, the first decision is which guard receives the inbound. That first pass starts everything in motion.

Phase 2 — Inbound & Release

1
1 receives the inbound and attacks up the right sideline with the ball.
2
3 releases from out of bounds the moment the pass is made. Sprint hard to the right sideline past mid-court.
3
4 drops straight down the center of the court. Tries to keep an open passing window and maintains middle safety.
4
2 holds the opposite wing position. Do not collapse toward the ball.
Structural Bridge → Phase 31 advancing up the right side — push forward, swing to 3, or skip to 5.

Phase 3 — Advance & Cross

1
1 dribbles hard into the press toward the sideline — drawing defenders and creating the pass lane to 3 ahead.
2
2 cuts across the key. Reversal option if the press collapses on 1.
3
3 is stationary on the sideline and reading for a lob ahead. Hold position and DON'T go towards the ball — let the ball come to you.
4
4 settled in the mid-lane. Interior safety net.
Structural Bridge → Phase 4Ball goes to 3 or swings back to 2. Either option pushes past the first line of pressure.

Phase 4 — Reversal

1
1 delivers a pass to 2 cutting back across the top, then continues running toward the right side.
2
2 receives from 1 and immediately drives up the opposite sideline. The press followed 1 — the opposite side is open.
3
3 cuts diagonally from the sideline toward the opposite elbow becoming the new pass back.
4
4 continues forward in the middle continuing to be an option to pass ahead.
Structural Bridge → Phase 52 advancing up the right side. Three options available: 1 trailing, 3 cutting to the elbow, 4 in the lane.

Phase 5 — Read & Decide

1
2 has the ball on the sidelines moving towards half court. Three options are available — read the defense before committing.
2
1 is now ahead on the sidelines. Option if the defense attempts a second trap.
3
3 is the reversal option — in case defense fully rotates.
4
4 is in the middle heading towards the offensive 3-point line.
Structural Bridge → Phase 6If 3 receives the pass at the elbow, the next action is an immediate drive left toward the basket while 2 and 1 fill the vacated spots.

Phase 6 — Attack the Basket

1
3 receives the pass back from 2 and drives toward the opposite sideline — the press cannot rotate three times.
2
2 passes and immediately relocates to the opposite offensive wing — available for a pass ahead and to attack the basket.
3
1 cuts from the sideline to be an extra pass back and get the ball across half-court. Provides the safety valve and keeps the defense from collapsing without consequence.
4
4 holds the mid-lane. If the drive draws a double-team, 4 is the easy dump-off pass for a layup or short jumper.
ResultThe press has been broken end-to-end. The team has a layup opportunity, an elbow jump shot, or a kick-out three. This is the reward for running the press break correctly.
